Behind the design Methods, assumptions & references +

Separate-library mode randomizes one selected codon per library while retaining all other input bases. Combinatorial mode replaces all selected codons in every oligo, using every combination of method components. Molar weights multiply across sites. Choose a position to inspect its amino-acid distribution. Combined stop probability is one minus the product of per-site non-stop probabilities, assuming independent incorporation. Unselected stop codons are not included in that probability. Designs exceeding 2,048 ordered oligos are blocked before generation; reduce the selected sites or use separate libraries. Amino-acid combinations count possibilities, not an experimental coverage guarantee. Reverse frames are translated from the reverse complement; ordered sequences always stay 5′ → 3′. For a reverse frame, NDT becomes AHN, VHG becomes CDB, VMA becomes TKB, ATG becomes CAT, and TGG becomes CCA in the input-oriented oligo.

Frame detection uses the standard genetic code and ignores incomplete edge codons. The first stop-free frame is suggested in +1, +2, +3, −1, −2, −3 order. A short stop-free sequence is not proof of the correct biological frame. Confirm against your coding sequence. Positions refer to this primer, not the full protein.

This tool substitutes codons in an existing primer; it does not optimize annealing temperature, flanking length, specificity, hairpins or PCR strategy. 22c, Tang, NNK and NNS retain all 20 amino acids, including wild type. Restricted and custom codons may exclude wild type. Single-codon methods assume equal incorporation of the bases represented by each IUPAC symbol; stops are included in the distribution denominator. Theoretical balance does not guarantee experimental balance.
